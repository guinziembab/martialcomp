# Participant Dashboard

## Introduction

The Participant Dashboard is designed for martial arts practitioners who participate in competitions organized through the MartialComp platform. This interface allows participants to manage their registrations, view their results, and track their progression.

## Accessing the Dashboard

To access the participant dashboard:
1. Log in to your MartialComp account
2. If your profile is configured with the "participant" role, you will be automatically redirected to this dashboard
3. Otherwise, access it via the URL `/dashboard/participant/`

## Main Features

### Overview

The main page of the participant dashboard displays:
- Number of upcoming competitions
- Number of medals won
- Total number of participations
- Registrations pending validation
- Total score and average score
- Statistics by discipline
- Performance charts

### Competition Management

The "Competitions" section (`/dashboard/participant/competitions/`) allows you to:
- View upcoming competitions you are registered for
- View the history of past competitions
- Track the status of pending registrations
- Discover and register for new competitions

### Participant Profile

The "Profile" section (`/dashboard/participant/profile/`) allows you to:
- View and edit your personal information
- See your practiced disciplines and associated grades
- View your grade history
- Update your profile photo

### Results

The "Results" section (`/dashboard/participant/results/`) allows you to:
- View all your competition results
- Filter results by competition or category
- See the details of each performance
- Download your participation certificates and diplomas

## Additional Features

### Notifications

The notification system informs you of important events:
- Registration confirmations
- Upcoming competition reminders
- Results announcements
- Schedule changes

### Training Tracking

If enabled, you can track your training sessions:
- Total number of sessions
- Sessions this month
- Sessions this week
- Breakdown by discipline

### Upcoming Events

Calendar of events you are registered for or that might interest you.

### Finances

Overview of your financial transactions related to the platform:
- Recent payments
- Total payment amount
- Pending invoices

### Shop

If the shop module is enabled:
- Recent orders
- Order status
- Quick access to recommended products

### Support

Access to the support ticket system:
- Create new tickets
- Track existing tickets

## Customization

You can customize your dashboard:
1. Click the "Settings" icon in the top right
2. In the "Dashboard Preferences" section, select the widgets to display
3. Rearrange widgets by drag and drop
4. Save your preferences

## Navigation

The sidebar allows you to navigate between different sections of the dashboard. You can collapse it by clicking the menu icon for more display space.

## Mobile Compatibility

The participant dashboard is fully responsive and adapts to mobile devices, offering an optimal experience on smartphones and tablets.

## Technical Support

If you encounter difficulties with your dashboard:
1. Check the FAQ in the Help section
2. Use the contact form accessible from the footer
3. Create a support ticket via the dedicated section
