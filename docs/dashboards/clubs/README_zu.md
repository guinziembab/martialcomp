# Ibhodi Lokubuka Lekilabhu

## Isingeniso

Ibhodi lokubuka lekilabhu lakhelwe abaphathi nabaphathi bezikhungo zezemidlalo yobudoda. Lesi sixhumi esihlanganisiwe sivumela ukuphatha amalungu, imiqhudelwano, ukuqeqeshwa nokuphathwa okuvamile kwekilabhu.

## Ukufinyelela Ibhodi Lokubuka

Ukuze ufinyelele ibhodi lokubuka lekilabhu:
1. Ngena ngemvume ku-akhawunti yakho ye-MartialComp
2. Uma iphrofayela yakho ilungiselelwe indima "club_manager", uzothunyelwa ngokuzenzakalelayo kuleli bhodi lokubuka
3. Noma, ungafinyelela nge-URL `/dashboard/club/`

## Izici Eziyinhloko

### Ukubuka Konke

Ikhasi eliyinhloko lebhodi lokubuka lekilabhu libonisa:
- Inombolo yonke yamalungu asebenzayo
- Ukwahlukana kwamalungu ngokwesifundo nesigaba
- Imiqhudelwano ezayo ehlelwe noma ephathelene nekilabhu
- Ukusebenza kwamuva kwamalungu
- Izibalo zezimali (uma imoduli Yezimali isebenza)
- Izaziso nezexwayiso ezibalulekile

### Ukuphatha Amalungu

Isigaba "Amalungu" sikuvumela ukuthi:
- Ubheke uhlu oluphelele lwamalungu
- Ungeze amalungu amasha
- Uphathe amaphrofayela, amazinga namalayisense
- Ulandele ukuvuselelwa kobulungu
- Uphathe izicelo zobulungu
- Uhlele amalungu ngamaqembu nezifundo

### Ukuphatha Imiqhudelwano

Isigaba "Imiqhudelwano" sikuvumela ukuthi:
- Udale futhi uhlele imiqhudelwano
- Uphathe ukubhaliswa kwamalungu emiqhudelwaneni yangaphandle
- Ulandele imiphumela nokusebenza
- Uhlele ikhalenda yemiqhudelwano
- Uphathe ukuhamba nokuhlela

### Ukuphatha Ukuqeqeshwa

Isigaba "Ukuqeqeshwa" sikuvumela ukuthi:
- Uhlele izikhathi zokuqeqeshwa
- Unikeze abaqeqeshi ezikhathini
- Ulandele ukuba khona kwamalungu
- Uphathe izindawo nemishini
- Udale izinhlelo zokuqeqeshwa ezilungiselelwe

### Ukuphathwa

Isigaba "Ukuphathwa" sikuvumela ukuthi:
- Uphathe ulwazi lwekilabhu (imininingwane yokuxhumana, i-logo, njll.)
- Ulungise izindima nezimvume zabasebenzisi
- Uphathe ukuxhumana nezinhlangano
- Uphathe izimali zekilabhu
- Ufinyelele imibiko nezibalo

## Izici Ezengeziwe

### Ukuxhumana

Amathuluzi okuxhumana namalungu:
- Ukuthumela imiyalezo yeqembu
- Ukwenza izimemezelo
- Ukuphatha izaziso
- Ukwabelana ngemibhalo

### Izenzakalo

Ukuphatha izenzakalo zekilabhu:
- Imiqhudelwano yangaphakathi
- Izifundo nokuqeqeshwa
- Ukudlulwa kwamazinga
- Imiboniso nokuqala okuvulekile

### Izimali

Uma imoduli Yezimali isebenza:
- Ukulandela imali yobulungu
- Ukuphatha ama-invoyisi nezinkokhelo
- Imibiko yezimali
- Isabelomali

### Iziqu Nezitifiketi

Ukuphatha iziqu zamalungu nabasebenzi:
- Ukulandela amazinga nentuthuko
- Ukuphatha amadiploma okufundisa
- Izitifiketi zokwahlulela
- Ukuqeqeshwa okuqhubekayo

## Ukulungisa

Abaphathi bangalungisa ibhodi lokubuka ngokwezidingo zekilabhu:
1. Ukumisa amamoduli abonwayo
2. Ukulungisa amafomu namafilidi
3. Ukulungisa imibiko
4. Ukulungisa izilungiselelo zezaziso

## Ukuxhumana Nemimwe Imimoduli

Ibhodi lokubuka lekilabhu lixhumana nezinye izingxenye ze-MartialComp:
- Imoduli yokuphatha amazinga
- Uhlelo lwamaphuzu lwemiqhudelwano
- Isitolo se-inthanethi (uma sisebenza)
- Uhlelo lokuxhumana

## Ukusekela Ngobuchwepheshe

Uma unenkinga nebhodi lakho lokubuka:
1. Bheka imibhalo enemininingwane kumhlahlandlela wokusetshenziswa
2. Sebenzisa umsebenzi "Usizo" otholakala kumenyu eyinhloko
3. Xhumana nokusekela kobuchwepheshe ngefomu ebekwe ngokukhethekile
