# Ibhodi Lokubuka Lenhlangano

## Isingeniso

Ibhodi lokubuka lenhlangano iyisixhumi esiphelele esihloselwe abaphathi nabaholi bezinhlangano zezemidlalo yobudoda. Linikeza amathuluzi anamandla okuphatha zonke izici zenhlangano: izikhungo ezixhumene, amalayisense, imiqhudelwano, ukuqeqeshwa, nokunye okuningi.

## Ukufinyelela Ibhodi Lokubuka

Ukuze ufinyelele ibhodi lokubuka lenhlangano:
1. Ngena ngemvume ku-akhawunti yakho ye-MartialComp
2. Uma iphrofayela yakho ilungiselelwe indima "federation_admin", uzothunyelwa ngokuzenzakalelayo kuleli bhodi lokubuka
3. Noma, ungafinyelela nge-URL `/dashboard/federations/`

## Izici Eziyinhloko

### Ukubuka Konke

Ikhasi eliyinhloko lebhodi lokubuka lenhlangano libonisa:
- Izibalo zonke (inombolo yezikhungo, abanelayisense, imiqhudelwano)
- Ukwahlukana kwabanelayisense ngokwesifundo, iminyaka nobulili
- Ikhalenda yezenzakalo ezizayo
- Imisebenzi yamuva nezaziso ezibalulekile
- Isimo samalayisense nokuxhumana
- Ibhodi lokubuka lezimali (uma imoduli Yezimali isebenza)

### Ukuphatha Izikhungo

Isigaba "Izikhungo" sikuvumela ukuthi:
- Ubheke uhlu oluphelele lwezikhungo ezixhumene
- Uphathe izicelo zokuxhumana
- Ulandele ukuvuselelwa kokuxhumana
- Uxhumane nezikhungo
- Uhlaziye izibalo ngekilabhu
- Uphathe abameli nabaphathi

### Ukuphatha Amalayisense

Isigaba "Amalayisense" sikuvumela ukuthi:
- Uphathe izinhlobo ezihlukene zamalayisense
- Ucubungule izicelo zamalayisense
- Ukhiphe amalayisense ngamunye noma ngeqoqo
- Ulandele ukuvuselela
- Udale imibiko yezibalo
- Uhlole ukusebenza kwamalayisense

### Ukuhlela Imiqhudelwano

Isigaba "Imiqhudelwano" sikuvumela ukuthi:
- Udale futhi umise imiqhudelwano esemthethweni
- Uphathe ikhalenda yenhlangano
- Uqaphe ukubhaliswa
- Unikeze abammeli nabahluleli
- Ulandele imiphumela ngesikhathi sangempela
- Ushicilele ukuhlela okusemthethweni
- Uqinisekise amarekodi nokusebenza

### Uhlelo Lwamazinga

Isigaba "Amazinga" sikuvumela ukuthi:
- Uchaze izinhlelo zamazinga ngokwesifundo
- Uhlele ukudlulwa kwamazinga
- Uqinisekise ukuphakanyiswa
- Uphathe ukulingana
- Ugcine irejista esemthethweni yamazinga
- Udale amadiploma nezitifiketi

### Ukuqeqeshwa Nokuqinisekisa

Isigaba "Ukuqeqeshwa" sikuvumela ukuthi:
- Uphathe izinhlelo zokuqeqeshwa (abaqeqeshi, abammeli)
- Uhlele izifundo namasemina
- Ulandele iziqu zabasebenzi bobuchwepheshe
- Uphathe izitifiketi nokuvuselelwa kwazo
- Ushicilele izinsiza zokufundisa

## Izici Ezengeziwe

### Ukuxhumana

Amathuluzi okuxhumana namalungu nezikhungo:
- Uhlelo lwezimemezelo ezisemthethweni
- Ukuthumela ama-email aqondisiwe
- Ukuphatha izincwadi zezindaba
- Ukushicilela izindaba kuwebhusayithi yenhlangano

### Ukuphathwa Kwemibhalo

Uhlelo oluphelele lokuphathwa kwemibhalo yenhlangano:
- Imithetho yobuchwepheshe
- Imibhalo yezokuphatha
- Ama-akhayifu emiqhudelwano
- Izinsiza zokuqeqeshwa
- Imibhalo yezikhundla nezinqumo ezisemthethweni

### Imoduli Yezimali

Uma imoduli Yezimali isebenza:
- Ukuphathwa kwemali yobulungu kwezikhungo
- Ukulandela imali engenayo yamalayisense nokuxhumana
- Izabelomali zemiqhudelwano
- Imibiko yezimali
- Ukukhokha ngokuzenzakalelayo

### Izibalo Nemibiko

Amathuluzi okuhlaziya athuthukile:
- Ukuthuthuka kwenombolo yamalungu
- Ukwahlukana ngokwendawo kwababambe iqhaza
- Ukuhlaziya kokusebenza emiqhudelwaneni
- Imibiko elungiselelwe
- Amabhodi okubuka angathunyelwa

## Ukuphathwa Okuthuthukile

### Ukuphatha Abasebenzisi

Amathuluzi okuphatha izindima ezihlukene enhlanganweni:
- Ukudala ama-akhawunti ezokuphatha
- Ukunikeza izindima nezimvume
- Ukudlulisela umthwalo ngokwendawo
- Ukulandela imisebenzi yabaphathi

### Izilungiselelo Zenhlangano

Ukumisa okuphelele kwephrofayela yenhlangano:
- Ulwazi oluvamile nokuxhumana
- I-logo nomklamo wokubukeka
- Izifundo ezisemthethweni
- Izinhlelo zamazinga ezamkelwe
- Imithetho yemiqhudelwano

### Ukulungisa

Izinketho zokulungisa ibhodi lokubuka:
- Amamoduli abonwayo nokuhlela kwawo
- Imibiko nezibalo ezibalulekile
- Izexwayiso nezaziso
- Isixhumi ngemibala yenhlangano

## Ukusekela Ngobuchwepheshe

Uma unenkinga nebhodi lakho lokubuka:
1. Bheka imibhalo enemininingwane kumhlahlandlela wokusetshenziswa
2. Sebenzisa umsebenzi "Usizo" otholakala kumenyu eyinhloko
3. Xhumana nokusekela kobuchwepheshe ngefomu ebekwe ngokukhethekile

## Ukuphepha Nokuhambisana

Ibhodi lokubuka lenhlangano liquka izici zokuphepha ezithuthukile:
- Ukubhala phansi kwezenzo zezokuphatha
- Ukuvikelwa kwedatha yomuntu siqu (ukuhambisana ne-GDPR)
- Ukugcinwa ngokuzenzakalelayo kwedatha
- Amazinga okufinyelela ahlukene ngokwendima
