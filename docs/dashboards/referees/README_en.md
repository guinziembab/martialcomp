# Referee/Judge Dashboard

## Introduction

The Referee/Judge Dashboard is a specialized interface designed for officials who evaluate and officiate martial arts competitions. This platform offers tools tailored to the specific needs of technical judges and combat referees.

## Accessing the Dashboard

To access the referee/judge dashboard:
1. Log in to your MartialComp account
2. If your profile is configured with the "judge" or "referee" role, you will be automatically redirected to this dashboard
3. Otherwise, access it via the URL `/dashboard/referee/`

## Main Features

### Overview

The main page of the referee/judge dashboard displays:
- Upcoming competitions where you are assigned
- History of past competitions
- Calendar of training and refresher courses
- Important notifications (summons, schedule changes)
- Quick access to regulations and scoring criteria

### Assigned Competitions

The "Competitions" section allows you to:
- View details of competitions where you are assigned
- See your role and the relevant competition areas
- Access detailed schedules and planning
- View the list of participants by category
- Download scoring sheets and specific regulations

### Technical Scoring

The "Scoring" section offers tools to:
- Access the electronic scoring system
- Enter scores in real time
- View evaluation criteria by discipline
- Review previous performances
- Analyze the consistency of your scores

### Combat Officiating

For combat referees, dedicated features include:
- Point counting system
- Penalty and warning management
- Integrated timer
- Injury and interruption tracking
- Interface adapted to different regulations (Taekwondo, Karate, etc.)

### Continuing Education

The "Training" section allows you to:
- Follow new rules and regulation updates
- Access educational resources (videos, documents)
- Register for refresher courses
- View your training history
- Prepare for referee grade advancement exams

## Additional Features

### Referee/Judge Profile

Management of your official profile:
- Personal information and contact details
- Qualifications and referee levels
- Disciplines and specializations
- Availability and preferences
- History of officiated competitions

### Communication

Tools for communicating with organizers and other officials:
- Internal messaging
- Schedule change notifications
- Availability confirmations
- Post-competition reports
- Questions about regulations

### Analysis and Statistics

For judges who wish to analyze their work:
- Scoring statistics by category
- Comparison with other judges
- Consistency analysis
- Evolution graphs
- Personalized reports

### Offline Mode

Features available without internet connection:
- Pre-downloading of competition data
- Score entry in offline mode
- Automatic synchronization upon reconnection
- Battery saving mode for long days

## Use During Competitions

### Preparation

Before a competition:
1. Check your assignment and the schedule
2. Download the specific regulations
3. Review the scoring criteria
4. Download data for offline mode if necessary

### During the Competition

During the event:
1. Use the real-time scoring interface
2. Quickly consult the criteria in case of doubt
3. Communicate with the area supervisor via the system
4. View other judges' scores (if authorized)
5. Efficiently handle incidents and special cases

### After the Competition

To finalize your work:
1. Validate all your scores
2. Submit your post-competition report
3. Report any incidents or improvements
4. View the event statistics
5. Update your future availability

## Technical Support

If you encounter difficulties with your dashboard:
1. Consult the detailed user guide
2. Contact the competition's IT manager
3. Use the built-in help function
4. In case of emergency during a competition, priority support is available
