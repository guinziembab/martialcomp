# Multi-Discipline Coach Dashboard

## Introduction

The Multi-Discipline Coach Dashboard is an interface specially designed for coaches who teach multiple martial arts disciplines. This platform allows efficient management of students, training sessions, and competitions across different disciplines, while centralizing all information.

## Accessing the Dashboard

To access the multi-discipline coach dashboard:
1. Log in to your MartialComp account
2. If your profile is configured with the "coach" role and you have qualifications in multiple disciplines, you will be automatically redirected to this dashboard
3. Otherwise, access it via the URL `/dashboard/coach-multidiscipline/`

## Main Features

### Overview

The main page of the multi-discipline coach dashboard displays:
- Summary of your students by discipline
- Upcoming training sessions and events
- Recent competition performances of your students
- Important notifications (grade examinations, upcoming competitions)
- Statistics and progression indicators

### Student Management

The "Students" section allows you to:
- View the complete list of your students across all disciplines
- Filter students by discipline, level, or age category
- View detailed profiles with grade history and results
- Track the technical progression of each student
- Manage competition registrations
- Organize multi-discipline training groups

### Training Planning

The "Training" section allows you to:
- Create and manage training sessions for each discipline
- Plan cross-training sessions combining multiple martial arts
- Track student attendance
- Prepare specific programs tailored to each level
- Set technical goals by group or individual
- Analyze the effectiveness of training methods

### Competition Tracking

The "Competitions" section allows you to:
- View the upcoming competition calendar by discipline
- Manage student registrations
- Prepare personalized competition strategies
- Analyze results and performances
- Identify strengths and areas for improvement
- Compare performances across disciplines

### Technical Development

The "Technique" section allows you to:
- Access educational resources for each discipline
- Create technical programs tailored to each level
- Prepare students for grade examinations
- Track technical progression by discipline
- Develop cross-training plans
- Document teaching methods

## Multi-Discipline Specific Features

### Cross-Discipline Coordination

Tools specially designed to:
- Identify complementarities between disciplines
- Create training programs combining multiple martial arts
- Analyze skill transfers between disciplines
- Optimize the overall development of athletes

### Certification Management

Centralized tracking of your qualifications:
- Teaching diplomas by discipline
- Specific certifications
- Continuing education workshops
- Mandatory refresher courses
- Renewal planning

### Comparative Analysis

Analysis tools to:
- Compare your students' performances across different disciplines
- Identify areas where certain training methods are more effective
- Evaluate the impact of multi-discipline practice on progression
- Create personalized reports by student or group

### Advanced Planning

Features to optimize scheduling:
- Peak performance management for different competitions
- Calendar harmonization between disciplines
- Periodized planning adapted to multi-discipline requirements
- Overall training load management

## Dashboard Customization

The dashboard offers numerous customization options:
- Widget organization by priority
- Default filters based on your main disciplines
- Definition of key metrics and indicators to display
- Creation of discipline-specific dashboards
- Notification and alert customization

## Integration with Other Systems

The dashboard connects with:
- Club management systems where you teach
- Sports federations for your different disciplines
- Video analysis tools
- Physical fitness tracking applications
- Communication platforms with students

## Technical Support

If you encounter difficulties with your dashboard:
1. Consult the detailed user guide
2. Use the contextual help function ("?" icon in each section)
3. Contact technical support via the dedicated form
